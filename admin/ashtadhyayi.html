Sūtrapāṭha enrichment coverage

Structured per-sūtra fields shown in the reader's ॥ पदच्छेद / विश्लेषण ॥ panel. Source: ashtadhyayi.com sutraani/data.txt + sutrartha_english.txt — padaccheda & anvaya come from the sūtra metadata, not from any single commentary layer. 👁 next to a field toggles it the same way as a layer above.

Coverage counts are read live from each layer's data.json. “Cleared (PD)” = public domain; “Permitted (curator)” = used under the ashtadhyayi.com curator's e-mail permission for non-commercial/educational use (source repo has no formal LICENSE) — keep attribution, resolve before public launch; “Verify” = not yet investigated against any public copy. Where a card shows a “Provenance evidence” expander, a real investigation happened and left a citable trail (exact source repo/commit, matched passages) — expand it to see exactly what was checked and what, if anything, is still unconfirmed. Licence resolution is a project-lead decision — this page only surfaces the status. Update, 27 Aug: Kashika, Nyāsa, Bālamanoramā and Tattvabodhinī were traced to a specific upstream (github.com/indic-dict/stardict-sanskrit-vyAkaraNa), confirmed public domain by byte-for-byte text match including reproduced source-file artifacts — moved from “Verify” to “Cleared (PD)”; the specific printed edition each was originally digitized from is still unidentified (does not affect PD status). Siddhānta-Kaumudī and Mahābhāṣya remain unresolved — no record of the claimed curator permission could be found in this repo beyond an unverified note, and ashtadhyayi.com's source repo still has no formal licence. This admin page's own layer paths for those two were also found and fixed — they pointed at a directory removed in an earlier taxonomy reorganisation, so this page previously couldn't load their data or status at all. Earlier, 23 Aug: the English gloss field (sutrartha_english.txt) was disabled and its data removed from sutrapatha/data.json — it was the one field shown to readers with no attribution at all. Vasu is unaffected by any of this.
