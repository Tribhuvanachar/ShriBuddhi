Support the scholars behind this library

Sarvamoola is built and maintained largely by volunteer effort. What you give here goes towards honoraria for scholars who proofread OCR, verify Sanskrit text, and help digitize new granthas — never towards anything else.

A trust is being registered for this project, but is not yet complete. Until it is, and until 12A/80G approval follows, contributions here are not tax-deductible and no 80G receipt can be issued — only a plain acknowledgement of what you gave. If that changes what you'd like to do, please wait, or write to sanatanavidyagurukuam@gmail.com first.
